Privacy Policy

1. Information We Collect

CocktailCouple collects the following information to improve your experience:

  • Personal profile information you provide (username, email)
  • Your home bar inventory and favorite recipes
  • Usage analytics and app performance data
  • Device identifiers and log information for troubleshooting

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the CocktailCouple application
  • Personalize your experience and recommendations
  • Analyze app usage patterns and performance
  • Enhance our services and develop new features

3. Data Storage & Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information. Your data is securely stored using industry-standard encryption and CloudKit services. Your home bar inventory and favorite recipes are synced seamlessly across your Apple devices through your personal iCloud account.

4. Third-Party Services

CocktailCouple does not share your data with third parties. We do not use advertising networks or sell user information. All your data remains within the Apple ecosystem.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy or how we handle your data, please contact us at support@dawnix.co.uk
