Privacy Policy

1. Information We Collect

CocktailCouple collects the following information to provide and improve our services:

  • Account Information: Username, email address (optional)
  • App Data: Home bar inventory, favorite recipes, personal collections
  • Usage Analytics: App features used, interaction patterns (anonymized)
  • Device Information: Device model, iOS version, app version for diagnostics
  • Crash Reports: Technical logs to fix bugs and improve stability

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information exclusively for:

  • Providing core app functionality (recipes, search, inventory)
  • Personalizing your recommendations based on your preferences
  • Detecting, preventing, and resolving technical issues
  • Improving app performance and user experience
  • Complying with legal obligations

3. Data Storage & Security

Your data security is our priority:

  • All data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest
  • User data is stored on Apple CloudKit servers (HIPAA-compliant infrastructure)
  • Home bar inventory and favorites sync securely via your personal iCloud account
  • We do not store passwords or payment information (handled directly by Apple)
  • Only you have access to your personal data; we cannot access it

4. Third-Party Services & Data Sharing

CocktailCouple has a strict no-sharing policy:

  • No data sharing: We do not share, sell, or rent your personal data
  • No third-party analytics: We do not use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or similar tracking
  • No advertising networks: Your data is not sold to advertisers
  • Apple ecosystem only: Data remains within Apple's secure infrastructure
  • No email lists: We do not send marketing emails

5. Your Privacy Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Correct: Update or fix inaccurate information
  • Delete: Request complete deletion of your account and data
  • Withdraw: Disable data collection in app settings

To exercise these rights, contact us at support@dawnix.co.uk

6. Children's Privacy

CocktailCouple is not intended for users under 13 years old. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided us with information, we will immediately delete such information and terminate the child's account.

7. Privacy Policy Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our practices or for legal compliance. We will notify you of significant changes via the app. Your continued use of CocktailCouple following changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

8. Contact Us

For privacy concerns, questions about your data, or data deletion requests, contact us at support@dawnix.co.uk. We respond to all privacy inquiries within 30 days.
